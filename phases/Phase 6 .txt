PHASE 6 — Adult Level Check and Child Literacy Check

Goal:
Build the adult level check and child literacy check.

Pages:
- /level-check-intro?intakeId={id}
- /level-check?intakeId={id}
- /level-check-thank-you?attemptId={id}
- /owner/level-checks
- /owner/level-checks/[id]

Adult Level Check sections:
1. Self Assessment
2. Vocabulary MCQ — 10 simple auto-graded questions
3. Sentence Building — 5 auto-graded questions
4. Reading Comprehension — short text + 3 questions
5. Writing — manual review
6. Speaking — audio recording/upload

Adult auto score placement:
- 0–30% = A0 Complete Beginner
- 31–55% = A1
- 56–75% = A2
- 76–90% = Strong A2 / B1 Activation
- 91–100% = B1 / Needs Speaking Assessment

Important note:
High auto score + weak speaking should not over-place the student. Owner final review decides.

Child Literacy Check sections:
1. Parent questions
2. Letter recognition — 10 auto-graded questions
3. Similar letters
4. Reading audio recording/upload
5. Writing upload/photo
6. Dictation upload/photo

Child literacy levels:
- Level 0: cannot recognize letters
- Level 1: recognizes some letters
- Level 2: recognizes letters but struggles with connections
- Level 3: can read simple words
- Level 4: can read/write simple sentences with support

Uploads:
- Audio: mp3, wav, m4a, webm
- Images/PDF: jpg, jpeg, png, pdf
- Enforce size limits
- Validate MIME types

Owner review page:
Adult review shows:
- Student info
- Plan purchased
- Intake answers
- Auto score
- Vocabulary score
- Sentence building score
- Reading score
- Writing answer
- Speaking recording
- Suggested level
- Recommended first lesson
- Manual score
- Final level dropdown
- Teacher notes
- Mark reviewed

Child review shows:
- Parent/child info
- Parent answers
- Letter recognition score
- Similar letter mistakes
- Reading audio
- Writing upload
- Dictation upload
- Suggested literacy level
- Recommended first lesson
- Final literacy level dropdown
- Teacher notes
- Mark reviewed

Acceptance criteria:
- Adult check saves answers and score.
- Child check saves answers/uploads.
- Owner can review and override final level.
- Recommended first lesson is generated or suggested.
- Level check status updates.
- Audit log records review.

Manual test checklist:
- Complete adult check.
- Upload speaking audio.
- Complete child check.
- Upload handwriting image.
- Review both as Owner.
- Mark reviewed.

Stop condition:
Stop here. Test this phase before continuing.
