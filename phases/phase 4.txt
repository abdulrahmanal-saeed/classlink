PHASE 4 — Checkout, Policy Agreement, Payment Status, and Thank You Page

Goal:
Build the checkout flow before payment and the thank-you page after payment.

What to build:
- Checkout page.
- Policy agreement checkbox.
- Payment redirect placeholder/integration structure.
- Thank-you page.
- Payment status rules.
- Owner payment review page.

Pages:
- /checkout?plan=single|monthly|bundle
- /thank-you?ref={checkoutReference}
- /owner/payments
- /owner/payments/[id]

Checkout fields:
- full_name
- email
- whatsapp
- selected_plan
- student_age
- learner_type: adult / child / someone_else
- main_goal: Speaking / Reading & Writing / Work Arabic / Kids Arabic / Emirati Dialect / Not sure yet
- preferred_contact_method: WhatsApp / Email
- policy agreement checkbox

Policy text:
“I agree to the Terms of Service, Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy, and Privacy Policy.”

Payment rules:
- payment_status starts as pending.
- If Ziina/payment link is configured, redirect to it.
- If payment link is missing, show clear setup message.
- Do not mark payment as paid just because the user reaches thank-you page.
- If no webhook/API verification exists, thank-you page sets status to pending_verification.
- Owner can manually mark paid/failed/refunded.
- Every status change goes to audit log.

Database models:
- CheckoutOrder or Purchase
- PaymentRecord
- Plan
- AuditLog

Thank-you page content:
- Thank you for your payment!
- Your Arabic learning journey has started 🎉
- Explain payment may be pending verification.
- Next steps:
  1. Complete student form
  2. Complete level check if required
  3. Choose lesson time
  4. Tutor prepares personalized first lesson
- Button: Complete Student Form
- Button: Choose Lesson Time disabled until form and level check are complete
- Optional welcome video placeholder

Acceptance criteria:
- Checkout collects data.
- Policy checkbox is required.
- Checkout creates secure reference.
- Payment status is not automatically marked paid.
- Owner can manually update payment status.
- Audit log records payment change.

Manual test checklist:
- Open checkout for each plan.
- Submit without checkbox, confirm blocked.
- Submit valid checkout.
- Open thank-you page.
- Confirm status pending_verification if no webhook.
- Mark payment paid as Owner.

Stop condition:
Stop here. Test this phase before continuing.
