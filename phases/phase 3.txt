PHASE 3 — Public Website, Pricing, Legal Pages, and CMS Basics

Goal:
Build the public marketing website and basic CMS foundations.

What to build:
- Homepage.
- Pricing page.
- Terms, Privacy, Refund pages.
- Articles listing and article detail.
- Videos listing and video detail.
- Testimonials display and public submission form.
- Owner CMS for articles/videos/testimonials.
- Homepage section toggles from settings.

Public pages:
- /
- /pricing
- /terms
- /privacy
- /refund
- /articles
- /articles/[slug]
- /videos
- /videos/[slug]
- /testimonials
- /submit-testimonial

Owner pages:
- /owner/cms/articles
- /owner/cms/articles/new
- /owner/cms/videos
- /owner/cms/testimonials
- /owner/settings/public-website

Pricing content:
Default plans:
- Single Session: AED 80 instead of AED 120, one 90-minute lesson.
- Monthly Plan: AED 640 instead of AED 960, 8 sessions / 12 hours.
- 30-Hour Bundle: AED 1600 instead of AED 2400, 20 sessions / 30 hours, never expires.

Pricing CTA:
“Start Now — Pay Securely”

Pricing flow:
- /checkout?plan=single
- /checkout?plan=monthly
- /checkout?plan=bundle

Add “How it works” section:
1. Choose your package
2. Pay securely
3. Complete your student form
4. Choose your lesson time
5. Start your personalized Arabic lesson

Add FAQ:
- Can I reschedule?
- Do I need to know Arabic before starting?
- Is this suitable for children?
- Will I get homework?

SEO:
- Meta title/description per public page.
- One H1 per page.
- Articles/videos have SEO fields.
- Public pages are indexable.
- Owner pages are noindex.

UI requirements:
- Professional academy style.
- Mobile responsive.
- Arabic/English ready.
- Clean CTAs.

Security:
- CMS protected by Owner role.
- Public testimonial submissions must be moderated.

Acceptance criteria:
- Public website works.
- Pricing buttons route to checkout, not payment directly.
- Owner can create/edit/publish articles.
- Owner can create/edit/publish videos.
- Testimonials require approval before public display.
- Homepage toggles work.

Manual test checklist:
- Open homepage mobile and desktop.
- Open pricing.
- Click all pricing CTAs.
- Create article draft.
- Publish article.
- Submit testimonial.
- Approve testimonial.

Stop condition:
Stop here. Test this phase before continuing.
