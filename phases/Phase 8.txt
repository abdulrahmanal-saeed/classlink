PHASE 8 — Lesson Credits, Packages, Attendance, and Balance

Goal:
Build core package balance and lesson credit tracking.

What to build:
- Lesson packages from purchases.
- Credit ledger/transactions.
- Used/remaining sessions.
- Attendance status.
- Cancellation/no-show rules.
- Manual credit adjustment by Owner.

Models:
- Plan
- Purchase
- LessonPackage
- LessonCreditTransaction
- LessonSession
- AttendanceRecord if needed

Session statuses:
- planned
- confirmed
- completed
- canceled_on_time
- canceled_late
- rescheduled
- no_show

Credit transaction types:
- purchase_grant
- session_deducted
- cancellation_return
- manual_adjustment
- refund_adjustment

Rules:
- Single Session grants 1 credit.
- Monthly Plan grants 8 credits.
- 30-Hour Bundle grants 20 credits.
- Completed session deducts 1 credit.
- No-show deducts 1 credit by default.
- Late cancellation deducts 1 credit by default.
- On-time cancellation returns/keeps credit.
- Rules editable in Settings.

Pages:
- /owner/packages
- /owner/students/[id]/credits
- /student/balance
- /parent/child/[id]/balance

Acceptance criteria:
- Package balance is accurate.
- Credit ledger shows every change.
- Owner can manually adjust with reason.
- Student sees own balance.
- Parent sees child balance.
- Audit log records all adjustments.

Manual test checklist:
- Create package.
- Mark session completed.
- Confirm credit deducted.
- Mark no-show.
- Confirm credit deducted.
- Cancel on time.
- Confirm credit returned/not deducted.
- Manual adjust credit.

Stop condition:
Stop here. Test this phase before continuing.
